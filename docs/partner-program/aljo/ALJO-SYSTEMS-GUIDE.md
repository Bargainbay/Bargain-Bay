# Aljo Systems — your Bargain Bay partner guide

DRAFT. Items in [brackets] are confirmed when we agree your rates. Questions: Service@rssolutions.ca.

## 1. What this is
You and your team send Bargain Bay warm leads and sell our appliances online. You run your own team; we run the sales calls, the
delivery and the paperwork. Your portal shows everything your team has done. Your people see only their own leads and what happened to them.

## 2. Your portal (sign in at bargainbay.ca/partner)
You can see:
- **Your team:** every lead generator, their code, their link, and how many leads they sent.
- **All leads** your team sent, with what happened: Sent, With our team, Called, In conversation, Quote sent, Sold, Completed, Not interested, Couldn't reach, Not counted.
- **Duplicates:** leads that were refused because someone had already sent them, with who sent the first one inside your team.
- **Quality per person:** how many of their leads we could reach and how many turned into sales.
- **Orders and commission** from your link, your people's codes and your resellers, the same way SecondShop sees theirs. We never show unit costs.
- **Resellers** who joined through your link.
- **Tonight's list:** new stock and what sold, for your team's Facebook posts.

## 3. Setting up your team
1. Open **Your team** in your portal, pick **Lead generator**, enter their name and email and press **Add a lead generator**. There is nothing to ask us for: it works straight away (up to 100 people).
2. They get an email to choose a password. Their login only ever shows **their own** leads.
3. Each person automatically gets their own discount code (for example `ALJO-ANNA5`) and link. Do not make codes up yourselves.
4. To remove someone, press **Remove**. Their login stops immediately and their code stops working for new orders. Leads they already sent stay on the record.
5. Only you (the Aljo logins) can add or remove people. Your lead generators cannot.
6. **Add people you know.** Each person is your responsibility: what they post, and how they treat the people they contact, reflects on you and on us.

## 4. Leads: the rules your team follows
Give your team the **Lead Generator Guide** and make sure they read it. The key rules:
- **Only send someone who contacted your person themselves** (a message, a call, in person). Never send a name from a list, a stranger, or someone who said no.
- **First in wins.** If two of your people (or anyone else) send the same person, the first one counts. The second is refused and the sender is told.
- **Phone or email must be real.** Fake or recycled details are marked as junk. Junk lowers that person's quality score and, if it continues, we will ask you to remove them.
- **Nothing counts until it is a paid sale.** A lead is not money. The commission follows delivered, paid orders.
- **Never promise anything** about price, delivery date or warranty beyond what's on bargainbay.ca. Our team confirms everything.

## 5. What happens to a lead (so you can answer your people)
1. A lead is sent (form or screenshot) and checked for duplicates.
2. If it is new, our team is told. John or Roushi takes it, usually the same day.
3. They call or message the customer. The status moves to **Called**, then **In conversation** and **Quote sent** as it progresses.
4. If the customer buys and pays, the status becomes **Sold**, and **Completed** once it is delivered. Otherwise it ends as **Not interested** or **Couldn't reach**.
5. Statuses are updated by our team's real activity. You cannot change them, and neither can your people.

## 6. Online sales with codes
Each generator's code gives the customer [5]% off online at bargainbay.ca and credits the sale to that person and to Aljo. The customer
types the code at checkout, or arrives from the person's link. If a customer already belongs to another partner they stay with that partner.
A sale made by someone using their own code is not counted.

## 7. Resellers
Resellers are businesses that buy regularly at reseller pricing. Send them **your reseller link** (shown in your portal). They create an
account and apply; we approve them. Resellers you bring are credited to Aljo. Only Aljo logins have the reseller link; your lead
generators do not. Reseller pricing is not shown to your lead generators.

## 8. Posting on Facebook (your team's daily routine)
- Every lead generator's dashboard has a **Posting kit**: ready captions, photos and their own link for every unit we have.
- They tick **I posted this** on each item they post. That's what makes the take-down list work.
- **Every evening** (about 7 pm) each person gets an email: their **take these down** list (items they posted that sold) and the new
  stock. You get a team summary. The same lists are on the dashboard all day.
- Remind your team: **sold means sold. Take the listing down the same night.** A sold appliance still posted wastes a buyer's time and
  is bad for all of us.
- Use the price in the kit. Do not change it.
- Facebook limits how much a personal account can sell and objects to identical posts. Each person is responsible for their own account's
  compliance with Facebook's rules. Use real photos where we provide them, vary the wording, and don't post the same item in many groups.

## 9. Money
- We pay **Aljo**, not individuals. How you share it with your people is your business.
- Commission is a percentage of **our profit** on each order (what we sold it for, less what it cost us, after any discount). A cheaper sale means a smaller profit and a smaller commission.
- Rates: reseller sales 15% of profit; sales with your people's codes 10% of profit; leads our team closes 10% of profit. [confirm before sending]
- [Commission is earned when an order is delivered and paid; reported on your portal and a monthly statement.]
- Refunds and cancellations reduce commission. A customer is attached to Aljo [permanently / for 12 months] once their first order is credited.
- You see sale price, discount, profit and commission for orders from your team. Your lead generators see none of this.

## 10. Privacy
- Customer details belong to the customer and must only be sent to us for the purpose of buying from us.
- Never share a customer's details elsewhere or send them marketing from your own channels using our leads.
- Screenshots your people upload are stored privately and deleted after 90 days.
- If a customer tells your person to stop contacting them, tell us and do not send them again.

## 11. If something goes wrong
- *A lead was refused as a duplicate:* this is normal. Check the status list; it shows who sent first inside your team.
- *A lead I sent never got called:* tell us the lead name; we check our queue.
- *A code isn't working:* confirm the person is still on your team list; contact us.
- *A sale isn't showing:* sales appear once the order is placed. Commission appears once it is delivered and paid.
- *A customer complaint about a posting or a call:* tell us immediately.
