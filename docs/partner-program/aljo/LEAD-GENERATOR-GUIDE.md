# Lead Generator Guide — Bargain Bay

DRAFT. Your Aljo Systems manager added you. Read this once, then keep it handy.

## What you do
1. **Post** appliances on Facebook (we give you the photos and words).
2. **Send leads:** when someone wants to buy, or wants delivery or repair, you send us their details.
3. **Share your code:** your own code gives buyers a discount online and credits the sale to you.

You don't need to know our prices beyond what's on the post. You never see our costs or wholesale pricing.

## Sign in
Your manager adds your email. You get an email to choose a password. Then sign in at **bargainbay.ca/partner**. Save it to your phone's home screen.

## Sending a lead
Only send a person who **contacted you**. Examples: they messaged you about an item, said "I'm interested", asked for
the price or delivery. **Do not send:** someone you picked from a list, a stranger, someone who said no or "don't call me", or someone
you only think might want one.

**Option 1: type it in**
1. Press **Send a lead**.
2. Enter their **name** and their **phone** (or email). Double-check the number.
3. Pick **what they want**: buy an appliance, delivery, installation, repair.
4. Add a short note (e.g. "wants a washer, in Ajax, asked about Saturday").
5. Pick **how they reached you** (message, text, in person, phone).
6. Press **Send**.

**Option 2: upload a screenshot**
1. Press **Send a lead from a screenshot** and choose a screenshot of the chat that shows their details.
2. We read it and fill in the form for you. **Check every field, especially the phone number.** Fix anything wrong.
3. Pick how they reached you and press **Send**.
Nothing is sent until you press Send. If the screenshot can't be read, fill in the form by hand.
Your screenshot is stored privately with the lead so we have the record of the enquiry, and it is deleted after 90 days. Only use a screenshot of someone who contacted you.

## If it says "already entered"
It means someone else sent this person first (or we already know them). That's normal. It's not counted for you, and nothing is wrong with
your account. We don't tell you who. Move on to the next one. If **you** sent them before, it shows you where that lead is.

## What happened to my lead?
Your dashboard shows each lead's story:

| It says | It means |
|---|---|
| Sent | We have it; nobody has picked it up yet |
| With our team | A salesperson has it |
| Called | We've contacted them |
| In conversation | We're talking with them |
| Quote sent | We sent a price |
| **Sold** | They bought and paid |
| **Completed** | Their order was delivered |
| Not interested / Couldn't reach / Wrong number | It ended without a sale |
| Not counted | Refused as a duplicate or marked junk |

You can't change these. They update as our team works the lead.

## Posting on Facebook
1. Open **Posting kit** on your dashboard. Every item we have for sale is listed with a ready title, words and photos.
2. Download the photos, post on **your own** Facebook Marketplace, and paste the words.
3. **Don't change the price, the condition, or the warranty.** Don't promise a delivery date. Our team confirms those.
4. When a buyer messages you: send them your link or code, or send us their details as a lead.
5. Press **I posted this** on each item you post.

**Every night** you get an email at about 7 pm with:
- **Take these down:** items you posted that are sold. Please delete those listings **that night**.
- **New items:** new stock to post.

The same lists are on your dashboard all day. Before posting something, refresh the page: stock changes during the day.

**Facebook rules are your responsibility:** use real photos when we have them, don't post the same item over and over or in many groups, and keep to
Facebook's rules for selling.

## Your code and link
You have your own code (like `ALJO-ANNA`) and link. When a customer uses the code at checkout, or arrives from your link and buys, the
sale is credited to you. Tell buyers: "Use my code at bargainbay.ca for a discount." Using your own code on your own order doesn't count.

## Rules
- Only people who **contacted you**.
- Real phone numbers and emails only. Junk lowers your quality score and may get you removed.
- Never promise price, delivery or warranty beyond what's on the post.
- Never pass a customer's details to anyone else.
- Take sold listings down the same night.
- If a customer says "stop contacting me", tell your manager and don't send them again.
