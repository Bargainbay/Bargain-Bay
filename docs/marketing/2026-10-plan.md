We have plenty of stock that is already 30 to 68% under retail, and nobody outside the warehouse knows. So the month is not "invent discounts". It is **a themed deal every week, a few well-capped codes, and proof that the units are real**.

4. **Trade & Contractor Week**, Oct 21 to 25 (pushes member pricing)

### Guardrails (please read before launching)
- **I do not have unit costs.** The feed shows prices only. Before any code goes live, each hero unit needs a check against cost (members must never go under cost + 10%, and consigned `VD-` units never under cost + 20%). Do **not** put `VD-` units (the LG ones) in a Drop of the Week; `/api/admin/clearance` refuses them anyway.
- Coupons are cart-wide (the system cannot limit a code to one category) and are date-based, not hour-based. `exclude_clearance` is on everywhere because the aged units are already at their deepest price.
- **Not verified:** whether a coupon stacks on a member's price. Test one order before Oct 21.
- Delivery is a $79 pass-through cost and coupons come off goods only, so there is no "free delivery" offer here. If you want one, it needs a code change.
- "Was" prices must be the genuine manufacturer list price. No "up to X% off" without a unit that really is X% off today.
- **CASL:** email and SMS go to consented people only. The opt-in box is never pre-ticked. SMS marketing goes out on the marketing number (`TWILIO_MARKETING_FROM`), not the driver number.

## Content pillars (rough mix)
- **Deals, 35%:** real units with the retail price struck through.
- **Proof, 25%:** power-on clips, serial plates, delivery photos, Google review QR. The most valuable sentence on a used-appliance listing is "this is the actual unit".
- **Education, 20%:** sizing, counter-depth, "what to check on open-box".
- **Warehouse and local, 15%:** the building, the crew, Pickering/Durham/Scarborough delivery.
- **Seasonal, 5%:** greetings with no sales language.
